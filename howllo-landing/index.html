How it works

Collect. Prioritize. Ship.

Three steps, one continuous loop your whole team and your users can see.

1

Collect the signal

Point users to your boards. They post requests and bugs — no account friction, just a workspace sign-in.

2

Prioritize with votes

Watch the top requests surface. Tag, dedupe, and set status while followers stay in the loop.

3

Ship in the open

Move a post to Done and it lands on the public roadmap and changelog — closing the loop automatically.
